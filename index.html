Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Please read this policy carefully to understand how Lo Studio handles your information. This policy may change over time. Continuing to use the site after a change is published means you accept the updated policy, so check back here periodically.

Lo Studio, Inc. ("Lo Studio," "we," or "us") is a United States corporation taxed as an S corporation. This policy describes the information we collect from visitors to lostudio.net and its subdomains (the "Site"), how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

01Information we collect

We collect two kinds of information: what you give us directly, and what is recorded automatically as you browse.

Information you provide

  • Your name, email address, company, and the contents of your message when you use a contact form or email us.
  • Your name, email address, and any details you enter when you book a call through our scheduling tool.
  • Your email address if you subscribe to studio updates.
  • Records of correspondence between you and Lo Studio.

Information collected automatically

  • Your IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, and screen size.
  • Pages you view, how you arrived at the Site, and how long you stay.
  • Aggregate usage patterns gathered through cookies and similar technologies.

This automatic information is not used to identify you personally. We use it to understand which work resonates, fix problems, and improve the Site.

02Cookies and analytics

The Site uses cookies, small files stored by your browser, along with a privacy conscious analytics service to measure traffic in aggregate. You can refuse or delete cookies through your browser settings. Some parts of the Site may not work correctly if you do.

Scheduling and newsletter tools embedded on the Site may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies. We do not run advertising trackers and we do not sell your information.

03How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss potential work.
  • To schedule and prepare for calls you book.
  • To send studio updates you have asked to receive. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site.
  • To meet legal, accounting, and tax obligations.
  • For any other purpose you consent to at the time you share the information.

04How we share it

Lo Studio does not sell or rent personal information. We share it only in these circumstances:

  • With service providers who help run the studio, including email, scheduling, analytics, hosting and accounting, who may only use it to provide those services to us.
  • With a buyer or successor if the business is merged, acquired, restructured, or dissolved.
  • When required by law, court order, or a legitimate government or regulatory request.
  • When necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Lo Studio, our clients, or others.
  • With your consent.

We may share aggregated or anonymized information that does not identify any individual without restriction.

05Client work and confidentiality

Project materials we receive from clients are governed by our agreement with that client, not by this policy. We treat client materials as confidential and publish case studies only with permission.

06Retention

We keep inquiries and correspondence for as long as needed to serve the relationship and satisfy legal or tax requirements, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your information at any time using the contact link below.

07Your choices and rights

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Unsubscribe from studio updates at any time.
  • Set your browser to refuse or clear cookies.

To make a request, book a call and we will take it from there.

08Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09Children

The Site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

10Visitors outside the United States

Lo Studio operates in the United States. If you access the Site from a region with different data protection laws, be aware that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, and by using the Site you consent to that transfer.

11Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date at the top. Material changes will be noted clearly.

12Contact

Questions about this policy or our practices: book a call.
